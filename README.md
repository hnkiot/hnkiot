# HNK IoT Solutions

Embedded systems, IoT and software, from the circuit board to the cloud. A Cape Town based engineering company, registered with the CIPC in South Africa. Engineer led, not a reseller: the same team that designs the circuit and writes the firmware also builds the cloud software on top of it.

**What we do**
- Embedded systems and firmware (ESP32, STM32, Arduino)
- IoT and telemetry (MQTT, LoRaWAN, cellular)
- Software and dashboards (React, Python, C#, Node)
- Electronics and prototyping
- Technical support and networking
- Energy monitoring (solar, battery storage, smart metering)
- Engineering training and mentoring

Working with clients across South Africa and internationally, in English and French.
